# v1.154.649 (2026-08-24)

feat(research,exhibition): wire four surfaces that were built and never routed - #1481. THE RESEARCH TOOLS SIDEBAR advertised three features and all three links returned 404: Source Assessment, Annotation Studio and Trust Score. Their views existed and were substantial, their tables existed (research_source_assessment, research_annotation, research_quality_metric), and for annotations the store/update/destroy routes existed too - nobody had ever written the page routes or controller actions. All three now load and are covered by tests/e2e/talk/research-tools.spec.ts, which drives them through a browser rather than trusting a reading of the code. SOURCE ASSESSMENT needed four columns: of its seven form fields only source_type and completeness had one, so provenance, authenticity_notes, reliability and bias are added, named exactly as the form names them per the rule applied throughout #1478. bias is deliberately NOT folded into the existing bias_context - bias is a grade (none|low|moderate|high|extreme), bias_context is prose explaining it, and collapsing a grade into a text field loses it and makes it unqueryable. One row per (object, researcher), updated in place: an assessment is a current judgement, not a log. Verified end to end through the real form - primary / partial / reliability 4 / bias moderate / provenance text all persisted and re-rendered. TRUST SCORE had a fabricated column: research_quality_metric holds metric_type and metric_value, no metric_name and no weight at all, and nothing in the platform weights these metrics, so the Weight column could only ever render a dash. Replaced with source_service - which service produced the figure, which is real and is the provenance question a researcher actually asks of a trust metric. Same principle as removing Max Items in #1477. The three dimensions are derived from what the platform knows (a recorded assessment, the description's completeness, whether any quality metric exists) rather than from a stored score, because nothing stores one - NOTE those weightings are my heuristic, documented in the code, not an approved specification, and should be reviewed before the page is put in front of researchers. STORYLINE STOPS needed more than a route. The Add Stop form posted to a GET-only route and raised a 405, nothing anywhere wrote exhibition_storyline_stop, the action never passed $stops so the list rendered empty on every storyline, three of four field names matched no column, and exhibition_object_id is NOT NULL with a foreign key - a stop is a stop AT an object, which the form never collected. Added the POST route, the controller action, the service writer and an object selector; aligned narrative_content to narrative_text, duration_seconds to audio_duration_seconds and stop_order to sequence_order; sequence_order defaults to the end of the route. Verified 2 to 3 stops with every field persisted. TWO ORPHAN DUPLICATE VIEWS DELETED: ahg-cart/admin-settings and ahg-core/add-digital-object, both duplicates of views that exist and work elsewhere (marketplace::admin-settings, ahg-io-manage::add-digital-object), zero references anywhere. Mirrored to database/core/07_research_tools_columns.sql, guarded, verified loading twice against heratio_test. Baseline 70 to 67. Left alone as agreed: the heritage analytics-search stub route and the display_as_compound decision. 242 Feature tests pass.
