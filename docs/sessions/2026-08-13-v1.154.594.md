# v1.154.594 (2026-08-13)

feat(exhibition): #1139 interior wall editor polish. Interior dividers were inert lines you could only add and delete; they are now movable (drag the line), reshapable and rotatable (drag either endpoint), and carry a live length readout in metres on the plan and in the wall list. Both ends and new walls snap to a 0.25 m real-world grid, and an end within 0.20 m of square with its partner snaps flush, so dividers land parallel to the room instead of a fraction off. Handles stop listening while add-mode is armed, or they would swallow the placing clicks. No backend change: everything round-trips through the existing saveWalls endpoint, and wall ids are preserved across an edit (verified), so placements hung on a divider - including the '|b' back face - stay attached when it moves
