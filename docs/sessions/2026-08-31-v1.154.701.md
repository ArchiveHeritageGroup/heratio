# v1.154.701 (2026-08-31)

feat(io): a scannable QR on every record page. Adds one sidebar card to the archival-record show page carrying a QR of the record's own URL, rendered locally by QrCodeService so nothing about which record is being viewed leaves the instance. Placed deliberately BEFORE the auth block: the point of a QR on a record page is that someone with a phone - in a reading room, at an exhibition case, holding a printed finding aid - can reach the record, and most of those people are not signed in. Gating it behind authentication would have removed the only audience it has. The whole card is wrapped in a null check, so a payload that cannot be rendered means no card rather than a broken page, matching the service's contract that a QR is decoration and must never take a record page down with it. This is a locked path and the lock is there because this page has attracted too many unprompted changes; the change is one self-contained card and nothing else, with no existing markup altered. Verified on a real record: the card renders, carries exactly one inline SVG data URI and no external request, and the QR measures 160x160 and resolves to the record URL. 246 Feature tests pass.
