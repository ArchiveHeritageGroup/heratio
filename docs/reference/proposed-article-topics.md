## Proposed batch (2026-08-15)

### Continuing the "___ was never the hard part / governance" series
1. **The Scanner Was Never the Hard Part** - why digitisation projects fail after the images are made: duplicates, missing text layers, 1-bit/bi-tonal masters. Grounded in the national heritage delivery audit (client anonymised in all outgoing material) and the toolkit's Flaws-and-Corrections. **[DRAFTED - `docs/articles/scanner-was-never-the-hard-part.md` (+ LinkedIn); held for the next round, not yet published]**
2. **Born-Digital Was Never a Digitisation Problem** - born-digital is custody, not capture; OAIS/PREMIS/METS/PRONOM. From Digitisation Toolkit Part 4. **[PUBLISHED - blog #33, 2026-08-15; `docs/articles/born-digital-was-never-a-digitisation-problem.md`]**

### From the Digitisation Toolkit
3. **Digitising the Un-Digitisable** - audio, video and 3D objects, and why A/V cannot wait (IASA TC-04/05/06 obsolescence urgency; CARARE/3D-ICONS for artefacts).
4. **AI Augments, the Human Decides** - human-in-the-loop as a digitisation acceptance criterion (the A12 / provenance-and-confidence model).
5. **The Business Case Nobody Writes** - digitisation usually is not cheaper; justify it on preservation, disaster-mitigation and fragile/obsolete-format rescue, not cost saving.
6. **Appraisal in the Age of Abundance** - how to choose what to digitise first: the condition-driven, AI-assisted prioritisation model.
7. **A National Digitisation Policy Exists - Now What?** - turning policy into a fundable programme (Delegation of Authority, MOA/MoU, APP alignment; the NARSSA A6.1-A6.7 crosswalk).

### Security and sustainability (from the CHT and VMS work)
8. **When the Records Org Gets Hacked** - lessons from a lapsed-licence CMS breach (anonymised, defensive): backups off, premium plugins frozen behind expired licences, nobody accountable.
9. **Who Holds the Keys?** - secrets management and access-handover for small GLAM/heritage teams (vaults, collections, joiner/leaver control).
10. **The Vertical Software Beneath Your Archive** - why mission-critical GLAM/records software keeps getting acquired, and what consolidation means for long-term sustainability of the tools institutions depend on.
11. **Preservation Is a Separate Server** - why preservation masters must never share a disk with access copies; dark archive, fixity, ransomware blast radius.

### Visibility
12. **Invisible to the Machines** - why your archive does not show up in AI answers, and the GEO fix. See [[project_geo_seo_exposure]].
