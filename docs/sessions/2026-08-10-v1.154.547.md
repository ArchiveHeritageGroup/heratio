# v1.154.547 (2026-08-10)

refactor(c2pa): dedupe provenance object-resolution helpers into two Concerns traits. Services InferenceProvenanceService + PreservationTimelineService shared byte-identical tableExists(), loadObject() and resolveId() ($this->tableExists variant) -> ResolvesProvenanceObject. Controllers ProvenanceController + VerifyController shared an identical Schema::hasTable-guarded loadObject() -> LoadsProvenanceRecord. Two traits because the service/controller loadObject variants genuinely differ (tableExists vs Schema::hasTable, service pre-inits title/slug). Signatures preserved. NOT merged: autodetectBinary (C2paService) == detectC2paTool (ProvenanceRecordService) - same body under different method names, needs call-site renames. Verified: bodies hash-match originals, lint clean, consumers resolve from traits.
