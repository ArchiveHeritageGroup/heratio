# v1.154.671 (2026-08-25)

feat(tools): scan-form-fields - find form fields that nothing consumes (#1481). The other half of the #1478 scanner, and deliberately so. That one finds a view READING something its query never produces; this finds a form WRITING something no PHP ever reads - an input whose name appears in no validator, no ->input() call, no insert and no update. Somebody types into it, presses Save, and the value goes nowhere, with nothing erroring at any point. #1481 records why a separate tool was needed: the binding scanner detects a ?? chain and cannot see an unwired input unless that field is ALSO displayed with a fallback. The exhibition storyline form contributed exactly ONE finding to the #1478 baseline while actually having four misnamed fields and no POST route at all, and the federation peer form contributed two while having three. #1478's class-A count was a floor, not a total - this measures the rest. RESULT: 66 fields across 33 views, out of 414 POST form views scanned. Baselined and wired into the code-quality job as --check, shrink-only, no database needed, exactly like its sibling. WHAT IT SKIPS MATTERS MORE THAN WHAT IT FINDS. GET forms are excluded because a filter form has no writer by design. More importantly, any package whose PHP saves through a GENERIC request loop - all(), except(), merge(), collect(), or a foreach over input() - is excluded entirely, because those consume whatever arrives and a field name proves nothing about them. 28 of the packages here do that, and without the guard the raw number was 109 rather than 66: ahg-semantic-search alone would have contributed 9 phantom findings, since its config saves via foreach over request->except('_token'). Calibrated by hand before shipping rather than after, because the sibling scanner had already taught this lesson the expensive way with a creator_id false positive that lived inside a Blade comment. A tool that cries wolf gets switched off, and then catches nothing at all. VERIFIED AGAINST A REAL DEFECT rather than trusted from its own output: ahg-reports/report-spatial posts top_level_only and require_coordinates while ReportController::spatialAnalysis() reads topLevelOnly and requireCoordinates, so both toggles are silently discarded on every submission - and the handler never reads six of the form's other field names at all. A pure case-convention divergence between form and handler, which is the same name-divergence shape as the whole of #1478. The gate itself was proved by injecting a field, watching --check fail with exit 1, and confirming it returns to clean on removal. ALSO AUDITED, and mostly already fixed by other work: of the seven surfaces on #1481, the storyline POST route now exists with correctly aligned column names, the cart admin-settings view is gone, source-assessment is rendered by ResearchAssessmentsController with a route, the ahg-core add-digital-object orphan is deleted, and the heritage analytics stub was implemented in v1.154.668. What remains is display_as_compound, still one view with no storage and no reader, left alone deliberately because it must CHANGE how children render rather than merely be stored.
