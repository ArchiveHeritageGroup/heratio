# Museum Workflow - licensing position

**Summary:** Heratio's Museum Workflow procedures (object entry, acquisition, location and movement, cataloguing, loans, condition, exit and the rest) are The AHG's own work and ship under Heratio's licence, AGPL-3.0-or-later. They do not depend on a licence from any third-party standards body, carry no third-party licence fee, and Heratio makes no claim of certification or compliance with any third-party museum documentation standard. Position confirmed by Dr Johan Pieterse, 10 November 2026.

## What this means

- **Licence:** the Museum Workflow procedures, their workflow definitions and the help text are part of Heratio and are licensed AGPL-3.0-or-later, like the rest of the AHG packages.
- **No third-party dependency:** there is no commercial or non-commercial licence to hold, renew or pay for in order to use, modify or redistribute the procedures.
- **No compliance claim:** product and marketing text describes the functionality neutrally - "museum collections management", "Museum Workflow", "a structured object-lifecycle workflow". It never names or claims conformance with a third-party standard or its trademark.
- **Customers and tenders:** a customer may use, modify and hand the procedures to another provider on the same AGPL terms as the rest of Heratio.

## History

- **July 2026:** the procedures were first built against a third-party museum documentation standard, and a commercial partner licence was considered so that Heratio could be described as compliant with it. An enquiry was sent on 17 July 2026.
- **October 2026:** that route was not pursued. v1.159.40 (19 October 2026) removed the third-party trademark from all visible text.
- **November 2026:** the procedures were redeveloped as Heratio's own Museum Workflow. The earlier "licence gap" no longer applies.

## Internal names

Some code identifiers (a package name, routes, a seed command, dropdown codes and setting keys) still carry the old name. They are internal names, not product claims, and are not shown to users. Renaming them is a separate code change.
