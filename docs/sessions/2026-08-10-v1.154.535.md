# v1.154.535 (2026-08-10)

refactor(integrity): dedupe 4 record-keeping services shared with ahg-records-manage. DestructionCertificateService, RetentionService, RecordDeclarationService and VitalRecordService were byte-identical copies (same integrity_* tables, same logic) in both ahg-integrity and ahg-records-manage - ~400 duplicated LOC. ahg-integrity's copies are now thin backward-compat subclasses of the ahg-records-manage implementations (the fuller records-management package; verified no circular dep - records-manage has zero AhgIntegrity refs). Consumers of AhgIntegrity\Services\* (IntegrityController, ahg-core IntegrityRetentionCommand) are unchanged. Added ahg/records-manage to ahg-integrity's composer require. Verified: all 4 aliases resolve via the container, are instanceof the base, inherit their methods (scanEligible/generateCertificate/declareRecord/getVitalRecords); no self::class/__CLASS__ usage so subclassing is behaviour-identical; lint clean.
