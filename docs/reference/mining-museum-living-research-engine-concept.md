# Mining Museum concept - "living research engine" + community hub (with concept-stage costing)

**Summary.** A concept developed for a client (a mine commissioning research into establishing a mining museum), reframing a one-off commissioned study into a **continuous, university-led, gap-driven research and virtual-exhibition programme**, growing outward into a **community-governed memory hub**. The concept is grounded in real Heratio modules (`ahg-research`, `ahg-exhibition` Lost Places/Reconstruction, `ahg-rdm`), with two honest new-build items flagged. A costed brief (v0.5) exists; a **formal presentation is still to be produced**. Audience framing to date: university, research and community partners. Illustrative composite disaster used throughout: the fictional *Kloofberg No. 3 shaft fire* (invents nothing about a real event).

## The core thesis

Gaps found by the research compiler are not a failure - they are the first output of a continuous programme. The collection and its virtual exhibitions are simultaneously the output and the instrument: each exhibition built reveals the next gap. The programme runs as a loop:

**Gap surfaced -> research question -> assigned to a researcher by level -> records created, enriched and deposited -> exhibition built from those records -> the exhibition reveals the next gap -> repeat.**

New research links back through one mechanism: a shared record identity plus a subject theme. A deposit becomes a record, the record joins the theme cluster, and the cluster feeds the exhibition.

## The community hub (grow into the community)

Design principle: do not build a museum *about* the community (extractive); build a community-governed memory commons the community deposits into, curates and benefits from, with the physical museum as hub and the virtual twin extending reach. Hub-and-spoke: physical hub + virtual twin + nodes (schools, community centres, unions, churches, sending-community institutions, the university). Angle groups: Contribute & co-create (memory bank with retained rights; co-curation feeding the gap-map; intergenerational transmission); Reconnect (SADC migrant-labour reconnection - "find your grandfather" across Lesotho/Mozambique/Eastern Cape/Malawi; diaspora reach; oral-history urgency); Learn (youth & skills hub; multilingual/low-bandwidth access); Recognise & heal (living memorial; contested memory; environment & land); Sustain (local economy; broader civic memory commons); Govern (TK/BC labels, CARE, consent; advisory group as co-authors). The standout, distinctive feature: the **SADC migrant-labour reconnection**.

## System status (what exists vs light build)

Shipped and demonstrable today: layered researchers/institutions/external collaborators; projects/assignment/outputs/DOIs/rights control; RDM deposit becoming archival records; virtual exhibitions + Lost Places reconstruction + 3D walkthrough; click-through from exhibition object to full record; evidence-coverage measurement; provenance integrity (inferred vs fact); AI transcription/translation for oral history (via the AI gateway); partial community/external contributor onboarding.

Light build (flagged honestly): Research Coverage / Gap Map; tighter research-to-exhibition bridge; community contribution & co-curation portal; TK/BC labels (per-region plugin); interoperability/persistent-ID/export hardening.

## Concept-stage costing (ZAR excl VAT)

Method: AACE International Class 5/4 concept estimate (order-of-magnitude, -30% / +50%), role-based rate card, bottom-up effort by function; firms to Class 3 after the inception phase. University research (master's/PhD/undergraduate) shown as in-kind - a cost the sponsor does not pay directly.

- First commitment (inception + pilot + one-off build): ~277 person-days = R1,748,500 labour; + pass-throughs R158,000; + 20% contingency -> point estimate ~R2.29m; range R1.6m - R3.4m.
- Recurring annual operating cost (steady state): ~R1.02m (R0.85m - R1.3m).
- Later phases (2-4), indicative order-of-magnitude, phase-gated, excluding the physical building: R8m - R15m over 3-4 years plus annual run.

The rate card is an indicative SA-market build; swap in The AHG's actual day rates and the model reflows.

## Governance and safeguards baseline

Editorial independence charter; mandate/ownership/custody/rights separation; research ethics + informed consent + takedown; collections policy; evidence-and-interpretation policy (fact/dispute/interpretation/inference labels); preservation and exit; audience/access; business, staffing and funding-portfolio model; research-to-publication quality gate; AI oral-history guardrail (originals stay authoritative); parallel physical-museum readiness assessment; interoperability/exit so the programme is portable outside the platform. The first investment buys a decision-grade pilot + governance package + evidence baseline, not a full museum build.
