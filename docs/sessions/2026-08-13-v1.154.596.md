# v1.154.596 (2026-08-13)

feat(3d): #1139 AR 'view in your room' on the record page. The object page rendered <model-viewer> with no ar attribute at all, so AR was available only in the dedicated /3d-model viewer - everywhere except the page a visitor actually lands on. Both live viewers on the record page (inline and fullscreen) now advertise ar with ar-modes='webxr scene-viewer quick-look' and a branded 'View in your room' button, gated on object_3d_model.ar_enabled; a plain GLB attachment with no settings row still gets AR. Also wires ar_scale and ar_placement, two columns that had existed unused since the table was created - ar-placement='wall' is what makes a framed work hang on the visitor's wall instead of standing on the floor. Verified the flag genuinely gates (ar_enabled=0 removes every AR attribute, =1 restores them on both viewers), which is the failure mode label_visible had. quick-look needs a USDZ via ios-src that we do not hold, so iOS falls through to webxr; the button only appears on a secure context, so it will not show over plain http on dev
