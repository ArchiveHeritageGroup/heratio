# How we use AI

This statement covers the software built by Plain Sailing Information Systems (PSIS) and supported by The Archive and Heritage Group (AHG): Heratio, AtoM Heratio and OpenRiC. It summarises our internal AI policy, AHG-AIMS-001, approved on 24 November 2026.

## Our role

We develop the AI features in our products and provide them to clients. When a client runs one of our products on its own infrastructure, the client decides how it uses those features. We remain responsible for documenting them, telling clients about known risks, logging their use, and telling clients about incidents.

## What we commit to

- We follow the law that applies to our AI work, in particular South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act, and the requirements our clients bring, including the EU AI Act where a client is subject to it.
- A person is responsible for every decision that matters. Releases, production changes and statements to clients are approved by a person, not by an AI system.
- AI output is checked before it is relied on. We never present AI output as fact without a check.
- We do not train AI models on a client's records, files or databases without that client's written agreement.
- Every AI system we run is assessed for risk and for its impact on the people it affects, starting with features that handle personal information.
- We assess the AI providers we use, and keep their data processing terms on file.
- We review our AI policy at least once a year, after any serious AI incident, and when the law or a client requirement changes.

## How we run AI

Our AI calls pass through one gateway that checks access, applies limits and logs every call. We prefer open-source models that run on our own hardware, and we check every model's licence before use.

## Standards

We have started an AI management system based on ISO/IEC 42001. We do not claim certification.

## Concerns and incidents

Anyone can raise a concern about one of our AI features, or report an incident, by writing to info@theahg.co.za. You can also log a call at https://callhub.theahg.co.za. We tell a client without undue delay about any incident that affects its data or its users.

Clients and auditors may ask for the full policy at info@theahg.co.za.
