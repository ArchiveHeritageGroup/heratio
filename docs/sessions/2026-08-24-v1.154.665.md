# v1.154.665 (2026-08-24)

chore(1478): regenerate the blade-bindings baseline at 8, down from 24. The baseline had not been regenerated after today's work, so the CI code-quality gate was guarding a ceiling of 24 against a reality of 8 - it would have accepted 16 regressions without complaint. --check reported every one of the 16 as fixed: and asked for exactly this, which is the shrink-only design working as intended rather than a surprise. #1478 is reopened: closing it left the remaining 8 tracked by nothing. NONE OF THE 8 IS A BINDING DEFECT. Every rename, dead alternate and unwired-query finding in the original population is cleared. What is left is six computed_data in the landing-page block views - the per-block-type resolver that would populate each partial's $data, a feature that does not exist - and two heritage analytics stubs whose controllers are one-liners returning empty arrays while the views loop entirely different variables. Those two arguably belong with #1481 rather than here. WORTH RECORDING ON THE ISSUE, and now is: the scanner cannot see the whole defect class. Two of the worst findings today were isset()-guarded rather than ?? chains and were therefore invisible to it - the function show page silently dropped five whole sections whose variables were looped and passed by nobody, and the declassification management page used none of the three variables its controller passed while needing two it never received. @if(isset($x) && count($x) > 0) is the same lie as ?? '-', and in this codebase it hides SECTIONS rather than single fields. Extending the tool to flag a looped-but-never-passed variable would find a second population.
