# v1.154.561 (2026-08-10)

refactor(research): dedupe project-context helpers into two Concerns traits. projectContext() (authorize + load project via Schema-guarded findProject -> [project,researcher], #1308 membership check) was byte-identical in 8 controllers (GrantEngine, ResearchFunding, ResearchOutput, MethodStudio, ResearchEthics, ResearchMilestone, ResearchTeam, Dmp) -> ResolvesProjectContext; context() (same but via a direct research_project query) identical in 6 studio controllers (WritingStudio, ClaimLedger, ArgumentBuilder, ContradictionEngine, ReviewStudio, AiDisclosure) -> AuthorizesProjectContext. Kept as TWO traits (exact bodies) rather than unifying, because the findProject variant has a Schema::hasTable guard the direct-query one lacks - merging them would change behaviour. DELIBERATELY LEFT (verified variants, no assertProjectMember): ReplicationPackController::projectContext + AnalysisBridgeController::context. Signatures/visibility preserved. Verified: 16 files lint clean, all 14 hosts resolve their method from the trait, the 2 variants keep their own method untouched.
