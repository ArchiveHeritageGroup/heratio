# Company names for Heratio (canonical)

**Use this wording for who builds and supports Heratio, AtoM Heratio and OpenRiC in public text: the README, documentation, help, the AI statement, blog bylines and generated output.**

> Heratio is **built by Plain Sailing Information Systems (PSIS)** and **supported by The Archive and Heritage Group (AHG)**.

## Usage

- **First mention** in a document: the full sentence above, or "Plain Sailing Information Systems (PSIS)" and "The Archive and Heritage Group (AHG)" in full.
- **Thereafter:** "PSIS" and "AHG".
- **Copyright line** on source files remains Plain Sailing (unchanged).
- **Owner:** Dr Johan Pieterse.
- **PSIS the company is not psis.theahg.co.za.** That site is a product demonstration. Where the two could be confused, write the company name in full.
- This note does not state a registered legal name. Contracts, invoices and other legal documents use whatever registered name Dr Pieterse specifies for them.

## Supersedes

This supersedes the instruction of 12 July 2026, which named "The Archive and Heritage Digital Commons Group (Pty) Ltd" (short form "The AHG") for all public text. Older documents still carry that name: update them to the wording above when they are next edited or reissued, rather than in bulk.

The same wording is used in the public AI statement (`AI-STATEMENT.md`), which summarises internal policy AHG-AIMS-001.
