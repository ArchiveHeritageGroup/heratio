# v1.154.721 (2026-09-05)

fix(privacy): a finding may declare a category of personal data only when something corroborates it. Three false assertions, all found by scanning the real catalogue rather than by reading code, and all the same shape: a pattern matching, a weak check passing, and the result reaching an Article 30 draft and a persisted retention proposal as though it were a fact about the institution's holdings. EPOCH TIMESTAMPS AS IDENTITY NUMBERS: Demo DO Fonds 1784035271814 is a millisecond timestamp, 2026-07-14 13:21:11, and it was reported as a VALIDATED South African identity number at 0.90 and had already drafted a National identifiers retention proposal. Its first six digits are 178403 and there is no month 84. validateSaIdNumber checked thirteen digits and Luhn and nothing else, and a timestamp is thirteen digits that passes Luhn about one time in ten - this catalogue had two. The first six digits of that format are a date of birth, which is a hard rule and not a convention, so a plausible date is now required. The year is two digits and only 29 February depends on which century, so a date is accepted if valid in either rather than guessing. Deliberately NOT checked: the citizenship digit, which is 0 or 1 on a real number. ahg:privacy-seed-demo-pii sets it to 2 so its records cannot belong to a living person, and rejecting on it would collapse the confidence gate that seeder exists to demonstrate - both its ID-shaped strings would score 0.30. Worth revisiting with the seeder, not alone. A BARE DATE IS NOT A DATE OF BIRTH: WhatsApp Image 2026-07-10 at 09.27.44 is a filename, and it put Date of birth on an Article 30 draft. The existing year bound could not help because that date is in the past. An archive is made of dates and nothing about one says birth rather than accession, exposure or hearing, so the surrounding prose is the only evidence available and a date without it may be shown but may not assert. The gate matches WHOLE WORDS and is windowed on the match offset, both learned from a sibling instance: its financial gate keyed on ref with a substring match fired inside reference, a word in nearly every archival description, so the gate meant to suppress false positives was open almost always. Windowing on the field rather than the match would let one mention of birth three paragraphs away legitimise every date in a long scope note. hasContextNear() is written to be reused - it is the infrastructure #1505 says to build before the ndpa and kenya_dpa patterns arrive, since neither has a checksum in existence. DEMONSTRATION RECORDS ARE NOT HOLDINGS: both accepted retention proposals on this instance traced entirely to the seed set, Phone numbers from four synthetic records and Payment card numbers from the published Visa test number in a fifth. ahg:privacy-seed-demo-pii labels every record [SYNTHETIC] with a banner in its scope note, so scanCatalogue now excludes them and reports the count as synthetic_excluded rather than dropping them silently. Excluded at the aggregation and NOT in the scanner: the per-record scan must still find PII in those records, because demonstrating that it does is what they exist for, and it still shows the 0.90 and 0.30 pair side by side. Matching is on the explicit markers and never on the bare word - archival descriptions legitimately discuss synthetic fibres and synthetic biology, and Demonstration against apartheid is a plausible real title, which is why the marker was not widened to Demo. The validated contract now reads corroborated rather than checksummed: true when a checksum passed or the prose supports it, false when that was attempted and failed, null when there was nothing to corroborate against. Across all 9,571 catalogue records the compliance scan now asserts no categories at all, and every one of the nine findings is review-only. 1091 Package tests pass, up from 1087, and 286 Feature tests pass.
