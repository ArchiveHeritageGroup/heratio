> Heratio Help Center article. Category: Research / Reports.

## Overview
Research reports document findings with structured sections based on templates.

## Creating Reports
1. Click **New Report** from the reports list or a project
2. Choose a template (Research Summary, Genealogical, Historical, Source Analysis, Finding Aid, Custom, and for museums Biographical Study, Object Biography, Provenance Research, Exhibition Research, Oral History Report, Collection Significance Assessment, Research Visit Report)
3. Enter title, description, and optionally link to a project
4. Sections are auto-created from the template

## Managing Sections
- **Add Section**: single, from template, or multiple at once
- **Edit**: inline editing with save/cancel
- **Move**: up/down arrows to reorder
- **Delete**: with confirmation
- **Comments**: collapsible per-section comments

## Templates
Managed at /research/report-templates. Each template defines sections with type:title format. System templates cannot be deleted. The thirteen system templates ship with every install. Their names, descriptions and section headings follow the reader's language, and a new report's sections are created in the researcher's language. Templates a site adds itself show exactly as typed.

## Export
Export reports as printable PDF with table of contents.
