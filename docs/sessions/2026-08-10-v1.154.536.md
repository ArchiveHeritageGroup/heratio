# v1.154.536 (2026-08-10)

refactor(api): extract duplicated ahg-api controller helpers into traits (AhgApi\Concerns). negotiateFormat (RDF content-negotiation, byte-identical ×3: ActorEntity/Entity/TermEntity) -> NegotiatesRdfFormat; paginatedResponse (data/meta/links envelope, ×3: V1 Actor/InformationObject/Repository) -> PaginatesApiResponse; primaryDate + creators + trimDate (event/actor meta resolution, identical across Citation/Mets/Iiif) -> ResolvesEventMeta (references $this->culture, which all three hosts declare). All 9 controllers now 'use' the trait; method signatures/visibility preserved so $this->method() call sites are unchanged. Verified: lint clean on all 12 files, reflection confirms each method resolves from its trait and the trait is in class_uses. NOT touched (genuine variants, different bodies): negotiateFormat in Catalog/Vocabulary/Graph, creators in Oai/Entity. Tail: identical trimDate still inline in Dataset/Entity (2 copies) - left to avoid dragging the culture-dependent trait into unrelated controllers.
