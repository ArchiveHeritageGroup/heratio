# v1.156.1 (2026-09-28)

docs(storage): help article and technical reference for the movement log (heratio#1514). The Physical Storage help article described a flat world that no longer exists: it now
  covers four tools rather than two, with how-tos for building the location tree, moving one container, emptying a shelf in one bulk move, and reading the history - including the two things people would otherwise misread, that an empty 'from' means a first
  placement for a container but the top of the tree for a location, and that a wrong move is corrected by another move because the log cannot be edited. Configuration covers the storage_location_type taxonomy and the staff-only permissions. New
  docs/reference/storage-movement-log.md records the data model and the decisions behind it for KM: why the log and the current-location index are split, why the foreign keys are RESTRICT, why a location move logs one row while a bulk move logs many under
  a shared batch_id, why user_id carries no foreign key, and the parity contract that any schema change lands on both platforms in the same release.
