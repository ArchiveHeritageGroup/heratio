# v1.154.593 (2026-08-13)

feat(exhibition): #1139 numeric display scale + on-wall label cards. Scale: Builder gains a numeric display-scale field (0.1-5.0) and an equivalent real-height-in-metres field, the two being one number since the walkthrough renders a piece at 1.5m * scale; Bigger/Smaller now route through the same applyScale and their clamp widens from 0.3-4 to match. Labels: printed museum-style card (canvas texture, world-space so it turns with the wall) rendered beside each wall-hung piece, gated on placement.label_visible - a column written by the Builder since #1138 that the walkthrough never selected, so the flag governed nothing until now. Card falls beside the piece rather than beneath when hanging too low to clear the floor, and title text is wrapped to two lines, ellipsised on overflow, with a character-level backstop for single unbreakable tokens
