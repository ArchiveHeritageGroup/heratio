# v1.154.532 (2026-08-10)

refactor(research): dedupe the copy-pasted ahg_dropdown/research_dmp option loaders into AhgCore\Support\DropdownOptions. dropdownOptions() was byte-identical in 7 ahg-research services (Dmp, GrantEngine, ResearchEthics, ResearchFunding, ResearchMilestone, ResearchOutput, ResearchTeam) and dmpOptions() identical in 3 (Ethics, Funding, Output); all 10 now delegate to the shared static helper, method signatures unchanged so call sites are untouched. Deliberately NON-cached (DmpService + ResearchOutputService are singletons - a per-instance cache could serve stale vocab in a queue worker). Verified: helper resolve(unknown)===fallback, delegating method===helper, lint clean. NOT merged (genuinely different): InboxService::dropdownOptions (4-col row-arrays not code=>label map), the actor/provenance CACHED variant, and inline ahg_dropdown queries in Decision/MethodStudio/ResearchMemory services.
